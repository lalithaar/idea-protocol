# IP-GB: Group Brainstorm

This idea is shared within a temporary group (the "Source Team") to encourage free discussion.

## Here’s what you need to know:

### What You Can Do:
- Discuss freely within the team: Talk about, refine, and modify the idea with anyone named as part of the Source Team.
- Use the idea for the current event: You can use the idea freely for the duration of the defined temporary event (e.g., a hackathon or a workshop).

## What You Can’t Do:
- No external sharing: Don’t share this idea outside of the Source Team during or after the event.
- No external development without notification: Don't use the idea to start a personal or business project outside of the Source Team without first notifying the Source Team and discussing credit.

## What’s Expected:
- Clear credit reserved: The idea belongs to the Source Team. Any external development requires the developer to **formally agree with the Source Team on how credit will be shared** before moving forward.
- Temporary use: The license is primarily for the temporary life of the Source Team.

## How to Use This License:
If you're sharing an idea under this license, just include a line like:

"This idea is shared under the Idea Protocol ([IP-GB](https://lalithaar.github.io/idea-protocol/v0.1/ip-gb)). For use within [Team Name/Event Name] only."

```

```
